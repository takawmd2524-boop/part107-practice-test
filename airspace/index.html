1. A beacon operating at a Class D airport during the day can indicate:

Answer: C. Operation of the airport beacon during daylight hours in Class B, C, D, or E surface areas often indicates ceiling below 1,000 feet or visibility below 3 miles.

2. Military Operations Areas (MOAs) exist to:

Answer: A. MOAs separate certain nonhazardous military training activities from IFR traffic. VFR flight is not prohibited, but pilots should exercise extreme caution when the MOA is active.

3. A Notice to Air Missions (NOTAM) contains:

Answer: A. NOTAMs contain time-critical information not known far enough in advance to be published, such as TFRs, closed runways, and outages.

4. One minute of latitude equals approximately:

Answer: A. One minute of latitude is approximately one nautical mile, which is useful when measuring distances on a sectional chart.

5. Class C airspace surface area typically extends to a radius of:

Answer: B. Class C typically has a 5 NM surface area and a 10 NM outer shelf, extending up to about 4,000 feet above the airport elevation.

6. Lines of latitude measure distance:

Answer: B. Latitude lines run east–west and measure degrees north or south of the equator. Longitude measures east or west of the prime meridian.
