1. Which factor increases the load factor on a small UA?

Answer: A. Steep turns, abrupt pull-ups, and turbulence increase the load factor, stressing the structure and raising stall speed.

2. Runway 27 is aligned with a magnetic heading of approximately:

Answer: C. Runway numbers are the magnetic heading rounded to the nearest 10 degrees with the last zero dropped. Runway 27 = about 270 degrees magnetic.

3. Which statement about the remote pilot in command's authority is correct?

Answer: C. The RPIC is the final authority for the operation (107.19) and must ensure it is safe.

4. An area labeled R-2508 on a chart is a:

Answer: B. Areas beginning with R are restricted areas. Flight within them is subject to restriction when active, and requires permission from the controlling agency.

5. What does 'see and avoid' mean for a remote pilot?

Answer: B. Remote pilots must use vision (with VO support if used) to detect and avoid other aircraft and hazards.

6. Radiation fog most commonly forms:

Answer: B. Radiation fog forms when the ground cools on clear, calm or light-wind nights, cooling the air near the surface to its dewpoint.

7. Operations over people in Category 1 require a small UA that weighs:

Answer: A. 14 CFR 107.110: Category 1 requires a UA weighing 0.55 pounds or less on takeoff and no exposed rotating parts that would lacerate human skin.

8. A microburst typically lasts about:

Answer: B. A microburst usually lasts no more than 15 minutes from first contact with the ground, with downdrafts that can exceed 6,000 feet per minute.

9. When is prior ATC authorization NOT required under 107.41?

Answer: C. 107.41 applies to Class B, C, D, and Class E surface areas designated for an airport. Class G (uncontrolled) airspace does not require ATC authorization.

10. An aircraft on 'final for runway 36' is:

Answer: C. Runway 36 has a heading of about 360° (north). On final, the aircraft is aligned with it on approach, flying north.
