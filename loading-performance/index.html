1. In a level, coordinated 60-degree banked turn, the load factor is:

Answer: B. Load factor in a level turn equals 1 ÷ cos(bank angle). At 60 degrees, cos 60° = 0.5, so the load factor is 2 Gs.

2. A heavier-than-normal payload on a multirotor will typically cause:

Answer: A. Motors must spin faster to generate enough thrust, increasing current draw, heat, and battery consumption.

3. A small UA with a total weight of 10 pounds in a 60-degree level bank must support an effective load of:

Answer: C. At 60 degrees of bank the load factor is 2, so the structure must support 10 lb × 2 = 20 lb.

4. If the center of gravity is too far aft, the aircraft will:

Answer: A. An aft CG reduces longitudinal stability, making the aircraft harder to control and more difficult to recover from a stall.

5. Flying at high density altitude requires a small UA to:

Answer: C. Thin air reduces propeller efficiency, so motors must work harder, draining the battery faster.

6. How does a steep turn affect the margin between flight speed and stall speed for a fixed-wing small UA?

Answer: A. Higher load factor in a steep turn raises the stall speed, reducing the safety margin above stall.
