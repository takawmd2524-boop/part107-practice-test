1. Which accident must be reported to the FAA?

Answer: A. 14 CFR 107.9 requires a report for serious injury, loss of consciousness, or damage to property other than the small UA when repair cost or fair market value exceeds $500. Damage to the drone itself does not count.

2. Under Part 107, may a small UA carry hazardous materials?

Answer: C. 14 CFR 107.36: a small unmanned aircraft may not carry hazardous material as defined in 49 CFR 171.8.

3. Before each flight, the RPIC must ensure the small UAS is:

Answer: B. 14 CFR 107.15 requires checking the small UAS to determine that it is in a condition for safe operation before each flight.

4. A drone without Remote ID can be flown without a waiver:

Answer: C. 14 CFR 89.115(b): UA without Remote ID may operate within visual line of sight and within the boundaries of an FAA-recognized identification area.

5. Under Part 48, small UA registration is valid for how long?

Answer: A. 14 CFR 48.100: registration certificates for small unmanned aircraft are valid for 3 years.

6. How soon must a remote pilot report an accident that causes serious injury to the FAA?

Answer: A. 14 CFR 107.9: the remote pilot in command must report qualifying accidents no later than 10 calendar days after the operation.
