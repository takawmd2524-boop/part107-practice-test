1. A PAPI showing two white and two red lights means the aircraft is:

Answer: C. The Precision Approach Path Indicator shows two white and two red lights when on glidepath. More white means high; more red means low.

2. Which hazardous attitude is reflected by 'Do something quickly!'?

Answer: C. Impulsivity is acting without thinking. The antidote is 'Not so fast. Think first.'

3. A remote pilot feels ill with a fever before a job. According to IMSAFE, the pilot should:

Answer: B. Illness is the first IMSAFE item. Flying while ill degrades performance and judgment.

4. Which action improves collision avoidance when using a visual observer?

Answer: A. The VO's role is to see and avoid other air traffic and hazards and communicate them to the RPIC.

5. A client asks you to fly over a crowd at an outdoor concert. What is required?

Answer: A. Flying over open-air assemblies requires specific Subpart D compliance (e.g., Category 4 or Remote ID requirements) or a waiver. Most drones cannot legally do it.

6. Wingtip vortices near the ground tend to:

Answer: B. Vortices sink at several hundred feet per minute and spread laterally near the ground, where they can upset a small UA.
