1. A temperature inversion is a layer in which temperature:

Answer: C. In an inversion, temperature increases with altitude. Inversions often trap haze and smoke, reducing visibility, with smooth air below.

2. Wind shear is best described as:

Answer: C. Wind shear is a sudden, drastic change in wind speed and/or direction over a short distance, which can cause loss of control.

3. A PIREP is:

Answer: A. Pilot weather reports (PIREPs) describe actual conditions such as turbulence, icing, and cloud tops experienced in flight.

4. A sea breeze typically blows:

Answer: A. During the day, land heats faster than water, so cooler air flows from sea to land. At night the pattern reverses (land breeze).

5. Which condition results in the highest density altitude?

Answer: B. High temperature, high elevation, and high humidity all decrease air density, raising density altitude and reducing performance.

6. The standard temperature lapse rate is about:

Answer: A. In the standard atmosphere, temperature decreases about 2°C (3.5°F) per 1,000 feet of altitude gain.
